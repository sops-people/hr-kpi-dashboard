How each KPI is calculated
Average headcount
(start + end) ÷ 2. This is the denominator for turnover and training rates.
Turnover rate
Total leavers ÷ average headcount × 100, for that month. Multiply by roughly 12 to get an annualised figure.
Voluntary turnover
Voluntary leavers ÷ average headcount × 100. This isolates resignations from dismissals, contract ends and retirements.
Time to hire
The average number of days you enter, from vacancy opened to offer accepted.
Absenteeism rate
Hours absent ÷ hours scheduled × 100. Decide once which absence types count (e.g. sick leave yes, holidays and statutory leave such as birth leave no) and keep that consistent.
eNPS
% promoters − % detractors. Range −100 to +100.
Training hours / employee
Total training hours ÷ average headcount.

Enter aggregated numbers only, never names or individual data. Very small teams can make individuals identifiable even from totals (for example, one leaver in a team of four), so keep that in mind if you share the dashboard (GDPR / LOPDGDD).
